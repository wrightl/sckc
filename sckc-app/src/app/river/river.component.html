River paddle sessions take place on Sunday mornings through the year, and occasionally on Tuesday evenings through the Summer months. They don't run every week - we plan each session when we have enough coaching cover, then email an invite to our members and everyone on our mailing list. Reply to the invite to book your place. All sessions must be booked in advance.

Sunday sessions start at 9am and typically run until about 12pm, with approximately 2 hours on the water depending on conditions and the group of paddlers.

The Tuesday evening sessions usually begin in May and run through to October. They start at 7pm and run through to about 9pm, depending on weather, available light and the paddlers. Bookings must be made by the preceding Tuesday to ensure coaches are available.

Parking

There is ample parking at our base at the end of Kelham Island, but parking charges now apply. You can pay by card, app or cash. Please check the signs when you arrive for the current charges.

Location

We are located at the end of Kelham Island. To get there, drive past Kelham Island Museum and through the large red gates at the end of the cobbled road. See the map below for the exact location.

What to Bring

While we can supply most of the equipment you'll need to get started, you will need to bring:

  • Suitable Clothing - ideally a waterproof top, and light clothing. Heavy clothing is not suitable because it can get waterlogged when wet and make swimming difficult
  • Suitable footwear - Ideally boating/slip-on/zip or Velcro fastening shoes. Please note that some trainers are not suitable if they are of a bulky design as they could prevent a quick exit from the boat
  • Warm, dry clothing to change into afterwards
  • A towel

We can supply the rest of the equipment you'll need including a boat (kayak or canoe), paddle, buoyancy aid, spraydeck and helmet.

Can I Bring My Own Equipment?

Yes you can! Please be aware though that it must be suitable for the occasion and will need to be checked by the a club official before use. If you're not sure please contact us, or add a note to your booking

Do I Need Experience

Absolutely not! Our aim is to introduce people to canoe or kayaking and to help you improve your skills

If it's your first time or you are unsure about venturing out onto the water straight away, then attend one of our Pool sessions first in a nice warm environment!

Awards

Our coaches are fully trained and qualified so if you would like to work towards the Personal Performance Awards we can give you the appropriate training and assessments, at a pace best suited to you.

Weather

All sessions are subject to the weather being suitable, and may be cancelled at short notice should this not be the case.
