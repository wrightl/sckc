About

Sheffield City Kayak Club is a friendly club who aim to make kayaking accessible to everyone, young and old. We can supply all the equipment you need apart from clothing and no experience is necessary.

We run club sessions on the river at our home on Kelham Island on Sunday mornings when we have coaching cover, and every other Tuesday evening from around May to September. We email members and everyone on our mailing list when a session is planned. We also have pool sessions every other Tuesday at Hillsborough Leisure Centre.

As well as our regular sessions we run regular trips around the country for beginners and more experienced paddlers on a variety of rivers, lakes and the sea in different types of boat.

We invite everyone on our mailing list to sessions and trips by email - just reply to the invite to book your place. New to the club? Start here to find out how to get on the list.
