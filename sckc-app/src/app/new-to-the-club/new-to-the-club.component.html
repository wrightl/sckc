Sheffield City Kayak Club is a friendly club that aims to make kayaking accessible to everyone, young and old. No experience is necessary and we can supply all the equipment you need apart from clothing. Here's everything you need to know to get started.

How to try it

You don't need to be a member to come along. All our sessions are booked by email: email us to say you'd like to give it a go and we'll add you to our mailing list. You'll then get an email invite for each upcoming session - just reply to one to book your place.

We prefer people who are new to the club to join us at a pool session before coming to a river event, so you can get comfortable in a kayak before heading out on the river.

If you're unsure about going straight onto the river, a pool session is a great first step in a nice warm environment.

Non-members can come to up to 3 club sessions in total, pool or river. After that you'll need to join the club to keep coming along.

What to bring

We lend you the boat, paddle, buoyancy aid, spraydeck and helmet. For the river, bring light clothing and a waterproof top, slip-on shoes, a towel and dry clothes for afterwards. For the pool, just a swimming costume and an old T-shirt.
